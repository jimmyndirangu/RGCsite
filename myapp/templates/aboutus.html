About Our Church

A place to grow in faith and love for Christ

Welcome to Redeemed Gospel Church! We are a vibrant community of believers committed to sharing hope, love, and the transforming gospel of Jesus Christ.

Our mission is to create a warm and welcoming environment where people can grow spiritually, find encouragement, and experience the power of God's love in everyday life.

Our Story

Redeemed Gospel Church was founded with a vision to build a loving family of believers rooted in biblical truth, prayer, and service. Over the years, the church has grown into a place where people can worship freely and find purpose in Christ.

Where We Are Today

Today, we continue to reach out to our community with compassion and faith, offering a place of belonging for individuals, families, and young people who want to grow deeper in their relationship with God.

Mission

To share the love of Christ with our community by offering spiritual guidance, encouragement, and opportunities for growth in faith.

Vision

To be a beacon of hope and inspiration, building a church family where people experience God's love and discover His purpose for their lives.

Whether you are new to the faith or have been walking with Christ for many years, we invite you to worship with us and discover God's purpose for your life.

Sunday Service

Join us every Sunday at 9:30 AM for our main worship service. Experience uplifting music, inspiring messages, and a warm community of believers.

Our youth fellowship also begins earlier at 8:00 AM to 9:30 AM.

Midweek Events

Every Tuesday we hold HBC services where we visit one of our members' homes and worship together in the evening.

Every Wednesday at 7:00 PM, we gather for an in-depth Bible study, and every Friday we meet for Kesha from 7:00 PM to 12:00 PM.
